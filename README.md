# Electronics Lab

Interactive, browser-based practice tools for a basic electronics class. Each
tool runs right in the browser with nothing to install, so students can use it
on a phone or a laptop. New tools are being developed and will be added soon.

**Live site:** https://chevellerob.github.io/electronics-lab/

## Tools

| Tool | Folder | What it covers |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Simple Circuit | [`simple-circuit/`](simple-circuit/) | A battery or 0–30 V power supply, a switch, and a bulb or motor with a fan. Animated electron flow (or conventional current), the same circuit as a picture and a schematic, what happens below and above a part's voltage rating, and a short check-yourself quiz |
| Engineering Notation | [`eng-notation/`](eng-notation/) | Beginner: regular numbers and metric prefixes. Advanced: decimal, scientific, engineering and E notation (4.7E-3), and metric prefixes from peta (P) to pico (p). Move the decimal point 3 places (or 1) and watch the exponent and prefix change; a prefix ladder with where each prefix is used; Windows Calculator tips; practice with worked steps |
| Ohm's Law | [`ohms-law/`](ohms-law/) | E = I × R with a short intro to E, I and R. A live circuit with a 0–30 V supply, ammeter in series, voltmeter in parallel and E12 resistors with color bands that overheat and burn open; the Ohm's law triangle with worked steps and units/prefixes; practice problems with ±5% answers |
| Batteries & Solar | [`batteries-solar/`](batteries-solar/) | Cells in series, parallel and series-parallel. Primary cells (alkaline, carbon-zinc, lithium coin, 9 V) with amp-hours, run time, what's inside a 9 V battery, and backwards cells; solar cells (0.5 V) and 18 V panels with a sunlight slider and shading. Live ammeter and voltmeter on a load resistor, and practice |

## Who it's for

Students in an introductory electronics course who want to see how circuits
behave outside the lab, and instructors who want a quick demo on the projector.
